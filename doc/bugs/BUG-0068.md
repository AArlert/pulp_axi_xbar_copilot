# BUG-0068 — must-read surface budget saturated; C1 fixer overstepped card authority deleting CLAUDE.md prose

## symptom

`scripts/tests/test_budgets.py` caps the "must-read surface" (`CLAUDE.md` +
`workflow/*.md`) at `TOTAL_BUDGET = 39500` bytes. Landing C1 (0.8.0's
follow-through commit) required inserting 66 bytes of mandatory operational
content into `CLAUDE.md`, but headroom before that insertion was only +8
bytes — the arithmetic does not close without removing something. The C1
fixer removed a 169-byte snapshot enumeration (`当前已改：FB-27（…）、
FB-28（…）。`) at the end of §5 to make room. That deletion was **outside
the card's declared authority** (a card is scoped to what it was asked to
add, not licensed to also decide what existing prose gets cut) and had not
been pre-authorized by orch.

signal: n/a (non-simulation; a budget-gate arithmetic dead end surfaced

time: n/a
how_found: the C1 fixer hit the dead end, reported it honestly rather than

## taxonomy

TB_BUG — process/governance gap in the framework's own budget-gate design:
the gate enforces an upper bound but supplies no disposition for what to do
once headroom is exhausted, so the choice of what to cut fell to the same
executor whose own card content was consuming the budget.

## fix

commit: none (no code change) — REV-038 §B.2 (rev ruling, this bug's own
disposition) is the closure record; `doc/bugs.md`'s BUG-0068 row itself was
edited in place by the same fixer commit that made the C1 deletion (no
separate fix commit exists for the governance question, only for the
byte-level content change already folded into C1's own commit).
what: rev arbitrated two questions in `doc/review/REV-038.md` §B.2 rather
than a code fix:
- **(a) ratify or reverse the deletion.** Ratified — the deleted content was
  (i) a self-declared redundancy (the same bullet's preceding sentence
  already states "该台账就是那份记录本身"), (ii) had zero live consumers
  (`grep -rn 当前已改 CLAUDE.md` = 0), (iii) survives in fuller form at
  `doc/fw-feedback.md:67,68` (both FB-27/FB-28 rows verified present), and
  (iv) was itself the exact kind of stale hardcoded snapshot F7 exists to
  catch (REV-038 §A's own F7 family). Deleting it *raised* invariant
  compliance rather than losing information — the ratification's own
  falsifiability condition (§E Q2) is: it becomes invalid the moment a live
  consumer of `当前已改` in `CLAUDE.md` is found, or `fw-feedback.md:67,68`
  stops containing FB-27/FB-28.
- **(b) structural relief.** FB-28's already-argued layering strategy wins:
  `CLAUDE.md` stays a gate-and-pointer layer; human-readable elaboration
  sinks to `.claude/skills/`/`CHANGELOG.md`, which are outside the budget's
  scope. Raising `TOTAL_BUDGET` was explicitly rejected as "a reviewed
  decision, not a fix" (the test's own docstring) reserved for the day the
  skill layer itself is exhausted — not yet, with 71B headroom remaining at
  REV-038's own evidence time and the skill layer largely unused.
